The 502 error indicates that the gateway did not receive a usable response from the upstream service. This can happen due to several reasons such as connection issues, unparseable responses, or the upstream service returning a 502 error itself. To diagnose the issue, check the `upstream_status` field in the edge access log. If it is `-` or `0`, the fault is likely in the service below the gateway. If it is `502`, the issue is with the upstream service, and you should investigate that service's dependencies. [adr-0019#1] [rb-checkout-502#0] [rb-gateway-timeouts#1]
